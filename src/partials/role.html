Role

Applying for Security Engineer at Bending Spoons. What the role asks for, and the work behind each point.

  1. 01

    Find security loopholes

    Across systems and infrastructure, with modern tools, automation and continuous testing.

    -> About 31,000 SCA, SAST, DAST and IaC findings triaged in five weeks, each one with a risk-based decision. See case file 2.

  2. 02

    Assess and protect company data

    Internal security assessments, and the countermeasures that follow from them.

    -> ISO/IEC 27001 risk assessment, controls and internal audit, from a 13% baseline to 94% of controls passing, and the PII and biometric data in the self check-in engine secured. See case file 1.

  3. 03

    Embed security in engineering workflows

    Security controls built into the infrastructure and into the way engineers ship.

    -> All the security functionality of a PCI DSS payment module, built myself and certified by a third-party assessor (case file 3), and a CI/CD security gate with no new SAST or DAST vulnerabilities in its first month (case file 2).

  4. 04

    Turn regulation into technical safeguards

    Working with privacy experts, from regulatory requirements to controls that hold up in production.

    -> PCI DSS and ISO/IEC 27001 implemented as working controls, and GDPR compliance raised from 21% to 96%.

  5. 05

    Track threats and share knowledge

    Emerging vulnerabilities and attack techniques, followed closely and explained to the rest of the company.

    -> At NTT DATA, frontman of the cybersecurity team and responsible for the human side of security; L3 tickets cut from 60 to 5 a month with Dynatrace, Splunk and Snyk. See the history.

  6. 06

    Work AI-first, in a small autonomous team

    A company that builds AI into its work and relies on small, talent-dense teams.

    -> An early adopter of AI, with engineering augmented through Claude that boosts my productivity. Open-minded, used to delivering with little guidance, and at home in small, talent-dense teams. See the description.
