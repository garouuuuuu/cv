Description

Cybersecurity engineer at Smartness, a hospitality technology company, where I cover what a startup CISO covers, without the title. Before that, three years at NTT DATA on system security for major Italian banks. MSc in Cybersecurity from Politecnico di Torino.

In every company I've worked for, I became the jolly, the wildcard people turn to when a problem doesn't belong to any single team. I adapt quickly and learn fast, and my knowledge runs horizontally across security, infrastructure and networking.

I'm an early adopter of AI and I adapt to its continuous innovation as it happens. My engineering is AI-augmented, mostly through Claude: it boosts my productivity and lets me run several workstreams in parallel. I stay open-minded about new tools and new ways of working.

Code shipped fast and iteratively will inevitably accumulate vulnerabilities if the process around it is not managed. I work and remediate at the same speed code is produced, building secure-by-default guardrails into the delivery pipeline so that velocity and security grow together.
